LLM Abstraction Pipeline

Seidman Cancer Center, University Hospitals · May 2026 to present

I am developing a secure LLM pipeline at our cancer center in collaboration with the informatics team and AI engineers. The pipeline converts unstructured clinical notes, pathology reports, and radiology reports into structured, research-ready data. The platform will also allow oncologists to iterate on extraction specifications and prompts, so clinical expertise shapes the output directly. We are validating output against a manually reviewed colorectal cancer cohort.
